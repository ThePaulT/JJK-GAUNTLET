# Three fights to establish the narrative standard

These are authored design fixtures, not outputs from a new simulator and not canonical battles. Outcomes are plausible paths selected to test causality. The future resolver must earn each event through eligibility, opportunity and consequence checks. Ability references inherit the provisional verification status in `roster-review.json`.

The first example uses an existing ladder boss. The second and third use Dagon, an existing roster character, to isolate domain and retry behavior; they do not change the five-boss ladder. Review whether the fights are clear, connected and worth replaying—not their numerical balance, which has not been tested.

## 1. Teamwork creates an ordinary victory

**Squad:** Nanami (uninjured Shibuya), Todo (two-handed Shibuya), Inumaki (pre-arm-loss Shibuya). **Opponent:** Hanami (Goodwill, uninjured). Neutral rubble-strewn arena. Neither side begins with an active domain. No off-roster assistance. Team members coordinate basic calls as an explicit game setup.

**Win condition:** a brief restraint and repositioning sequence lets Nanami land repeated close attacks while Hanami cannot cover all approaches. This fixture selects successful execution; it does not claim the trio always wins.

| Event | Resolved action and response | State/consequence |
|---|---|---|
| ORD-01 | Hanami drives roots through the approach; Nanami cuts free but is grazed | Nanami: material leg wound; approach blocked |
| ORD-02 | Todo identifies the root follow-up and swaps Nanami with himself | CE spent; rescue recorded; Todo faces the attack |
| ORD-03 | Inumaki's audible command briefly arrests Hanami during that commitment | Hanami briefly restrained; Inumaki throat recoil; voice unusable for the rest of this encounter |
| ORD-04 | Nanami uses the restraint to close and strike a Ratio weak point | Hanami arm/guard damage; no instant kill |
| ORD-05 | Hanami breaks the restraint and commits the damaged guard toward Nanami; Todo swaps the two allies | Hanami's response is spent; Nanami has a flank opportunity |
| ORD-06 | Nanami lands the follow-up; Todo supplies a simultaneous close strike through the opened guard | Cumulative curse-body damage reaches exorcism; squad wins |

### What the player reads

Roots tear across Nanami's approach, forcing him to cut his way out before they close around his leg. One catches him as he moves. Todo claps, taking Nanami's place just as another branch punches through the rubble; the exchange buys distance, but Hanami immediately turns to press them again.

Inumaki pulls down his collar. His command stops Hanami for an instant, and the recoil leaves him coughing blood. Nanami uses that instant to get inside the roots and drive his blade into a Ratio weak point along Hanami's arm. The curse tears free before he can finish it, dragging its damaged guard back toward him.

Todo claps again. Nanami disappears from that guard and arrives on the exposed flank, his next strike meeting Todo's from the other side. The accumulated damage breaks Hanami's body apart. All three survive, but Nanami is limping and Inumaki cannot force out another command; their next opponent will face a squad with fewer ways to create that opening.

**Carryover:** Nanami's leg wound, Inumaki's throat injury, each fighter's spent CE. No domain, Black Flash or unexplained healing is added. Inumaki's later recovery needs a logged transition; the prose does not pronounce a permanent loss of speech.

## 2. A domain overwhelms part of the squad

**Squad:** Nanami and Naobito (Shibuya before Dagon injuries), Megumi (Culling Game pre-possession). **Opponent:** mature Shibuya Dagon. All begin uninjured, with resources available. This is a hypothetical cross-time team using individually fixed versions. Dagon's domain may be opened when he obtains an opportunity; Megumi has the setup needed to contest it, not a full sure-hit domain of his own.

**Win condition for Dagon:** separate the defenders and interrupt Megumi's barrier interference. **Squad objective:** disrupt the domain and escape its sure-hit long enough to defeat its caster.

| Event | Resolved action and response | State/consequence |
|---|---|---|
| DOM-01 | Dagon creates distance with water, establishes Horizon of the Captivating Skandha and assigns Death Swarm targets | Dagon CE spent; domain active; per-target danger |
| DOM-02 | Naobito uses Falling Blossom Emotion; Nanami endures damaging attacks | FBE mitigates Naobito's incoming sure-hit only; Nanami gains a material shoulder wound |
| DOM-03 | Megumi establishes incomplete-domain interference and temporarily contests the sure-hit | Megumi CE/maintenance committed; suppression is temporary, not victory in a refinement ranking |
| DOM-04 | Dagon directly strikes Naobito while maintaining pressure, then lands a direct attack that incapacitates Megumi | Naobito torso wound; Megumi incapacitated; incomplete domain ends |
| DOM-05 | While Dagon is committed to Megumi, Naobito lands a close counter that critically disrupts the caster's domain maintenance | Dagon persistent body wound; domain collapses; burnout begins |
| DOM-06 | The survivors regroup outside the vanished barrier; Dagon remains active | Attempt lost: Megumi out; same boss continues with wounds and burnout |

### What the player reads

Dagon drives the squad back with a surge of water and closes them inside Horizon of the Captivating Skandha. Death Swarm appears at its targets. Naobito meets the incoming attacks with Falling Blossom Emotion, but his defense does nothing for Nanami, whose shoulder is torn before he can force his way toward the caster.

Megumi spreads Chimera Shadow Garden into the barrier. The sure-hit falters under the interference, giving the others a brief opening. Dagon answers with his own body: a direct blow catches Naobito through the distraction, and the next attack reaches Megumi while he is committed to holding the domain contest. Megumi falls, and his shadows begin to recede.

Naobito strikes before Dagon can settle back into control. The close counter leaves a deep wound and breaks the domain's hold; the beach vanishes around them. Dagon is still standing, but his technique is in burnout. Nanami and Naobito pull clear with Megumi out of the fight. If they continue, they must use the opening they have already paid for.

**Carryover:** Megumi incapacitated; Nanami shoulder wound; Naobito torso wound; Dagon body wound and fresh burnout; all spent CE. FBE did not protect allies or dispel the domain. Dagon's direct attack was not neutralized by anti-sure-hit interference.

## 3. The survivors exploit the actual damage

**Starting state:** exact end state of example 2. The player presses Continue with survivors. Clicking the button consumes no fictional recovery time. Megumi cannot act. Dagon has not regenerated, recovered his technique, or acquired new resources between panels.

**Win condition:** the two survivors finish Dagon during the remaining burnout window, using the persistent wound to compromise his physical defense. If they fail to create that opening, the resolver must permit Dagon to recover and turn the encounter; the word retry grants no bonus.

| Event | Resolved action and response | State/consequence |
|---|---|---|
| RET-01 | Dagon braces for ordinary close combat while his innate technique remains unavailable | No automatic water, Death Swarm or domain action |
| RET-02 | Naobito commits a short Projection Sorcery approach, forcing Dagon to turn into defense | Naobito spends CE; wounded movement remains constrained |
| RET-03 | Nanami times a Ratio strike into the exposed, previously wounded side | Dagon loses guard/body stability; Nanami spends CE |
| RET-04 | Dagon begins to fall out of stance; Naobito lands the follow-through before technique recovery | Cumulative damage reaches exorcism; encounter ends |
| RET-05 | Survivors end the encounter without healing or reviving anyone | Nanami/Naobito active and injured; Megumi remains incapacitated |

### What the player reads

Dagon braces as the two survivors close again. The domain is gone, and his burned-out technique gives him no swarm to keep them away. Nanami's injured shoulder limits his guard; beside him, Naobito keeps his approach short rather than pretending the blow to his body never happened.

Naobito commits first, forcing Dagon to turn and meet him. Nanami steps into the space that leaves behind and drives a Ratio strike into the side Naobito wounded when the domain collapsed. Dagon's defense gives way. This time, there is no barrier interference to maintain and no third fighter to rescue—the two survivors can follow the opening through.

Naobito's next strike lands before Dagon recovers his technique. The curse's body breaks apart under the combined damage, ending the fight. Nanami and Naobito remain standing, both injured and with less cursed energy than they started with. Megumi stays down. Their victory comes from the wound and burnout carried over from the first attempt, not from starting the fight again.

**Carryover:** two wounded survivors, spent CE, Megumi still out. The concluding explanation is an optional style choice: the final product can shorten it once the condition indicators communicate the same causal facts.

## Acceptance conditions before implementation is called successful

- Every named ability has a legal actor, target, activation and response in the event log.
- Every narrated injury, resource loss, recovery and casualty is backed by an event; environment-only detail cannot quietly create a new advantage.
- Domain establishment, interference, direct attacks, collapse and burnout are separate transitions.
- The second fixture's serialized end state equals the third fixture's serialized start state. No reset based on panel changes.
- Replay of a future implemented fixture is deterministic for its input and ruleset. These authored outlines currently have no simulated seed.
- The examples are not scripts the engine must force for arbitrary teams. Outcome assertions belong to fixtures with explicitly controlled opportunities and resources.
- One panel shows the entire narrative without act buttons. The user chooses whether to continue after the consequence is clear.
