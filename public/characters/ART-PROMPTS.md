# Art prompt pack — JJK Gauntlet

48 characters. Do **step 1 once**, then step 2 for each character you want.

---

## Step 1 — lock the style (paste this first, on its own)

> I'm making character portraits for a browser game. I need 48 of them and they
> must look like one coherent set, so first I want to lock a style.
>
> Style: modern shonen manga / anime key art. Waist-up portrait, character
> centred, facing the viewer or three-quarter. Hard ink linework, cel shading,
> limited palette — near-black background (#0B0B0C), bone-white highlights
> (#EDE9E3), and ONE accent colour per character used sparingly for their
> cursed energy. Dramatic low-key lighting from one side. Flat background, no
> scenery, no text, no logos, no watermark, no signature. Square 1:1, 1024x1024.
>
> Confirm you've got the style and I'll send characters one at a time. Use this
> exact style for every one so the set matches.

---

## Step 2 — one message per character

Paste a block below. Add the accent colour you want: **teal-green (#4E8F92) for
sorcerers, blood-red (#C4352B) for curses and killers** — that matches the app.

Save each result as the filename in the heading, put them in
`public/characters/` in the repo, commit, and they appear automatically.
Anything you skip keeps the generated art, so you can do ten now and the rest
later.

## Why these are written as archetypes

They describe each fighter's power and bearing rather than naming them. Asking
an image generator for a named Jujutsu Kaisen character usually gets refused,
and published likenesses of a licensed series on a public site are the rights
holder's, not yours. Written this way you get an original roster that reads as
the same universe. If you'd rather push for direct likenesses, that's your call
to make — just know that's the tradeoff.

## Specs that matter

- **Square**, 1:1. The app crops to square everywhere except draft cards (3:4).
- **1024x1024** is plenty. Shrink to ~512 before committing to keep the repo light.
- **`.webp` is tried first**, then `.png`, then `.jpg`. Any of the three works.
- **Filenames must match exactly** — lowercase, as in the headings above.
